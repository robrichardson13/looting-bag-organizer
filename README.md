# Looting Bag Organizer

##### A plugin for [RuneLite](https://runelite.net/)

Drag items around inside the looting bag's "View" window and they stay where you put them. The
game gives you no way to arrange a looting bag, so for anyone living out of one (Ultimate
Ironmen most of all) it's a jumble that reshuffles every time you add or remove something.
This plugin remembers your arrangement and applies it whenever you open the bag.

It is client-side only. Nothing about your bag changes on Jagex's end; the plugin just decides
which slot each item is drawn in. Your arrangement is saved per character and syncs with your
RuneLite account.

## How to use

1. Open the looting bag ("View").
2. Drag any item onto another slot.
3. That's it. The layout is saved and re-applied every time you open the bag.

## FAQ

**Does this move items in my actual bag?**
No. The game has no such action. The plugin only changes where items are drawn in the View window.

**Will my arrangement follow me to another computer?**
Yes, as long as you're signed into a RuneLite account.
